Open the prototype on a laptop (projector) or hand judges the QR code.

| Time | Click | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Page loads on Rohan's Split screen | "Meet Rohan, a 3rd-year ECE student at NIT Hamirpur. His squad, the Canteen Committee, lives inside Tata Neu." |
| 0:08 | Press **Play the week** | "Here is Rohan's week. Watch the phone and the live panel on the right together." |
| 0:12 | Monday | "Aditi splits a ₹960 canteen dinner. One push, two taps, ₹240 settled through Neu UPI, 2 NeuCoins earned and capped." |
| 0:22 | Wednesday | "No purchase intent at all. A quest nudge says ₹340 left in his food cap, and he opens Neu anyway." |
| 0:32 | Friday | "He logs the cab to Una station, the squad auto-saves ₹240 each, and the Goa jar hits 62%. That jar books Air India Express and IHCL Ginger inside Neu." |
| 0:42 | Saturday | "Sneha's poll surfaces Tata CLiQ and Westside picks, only when the squad is deciding." |
| 0:50 | Sunday | "Sunday Scorecard: 2nd of 18 squads at NIT Hamirpur, streak 7, next quest built from their own data." |
| 0:56 | Click **Ananya Iyer** in the persona switch | "Same feature, different life: Ananya, an SRCC day scholar in Delhi. Her quest is a café cap and her jar is a Jaipur trip." |

If something stalls, press the reset button (circular arrow) to return to the default view.
